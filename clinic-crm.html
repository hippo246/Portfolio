Project Overview

A clinic-oriented management project designed around healthcare administration workflows. This prototype explores patient records, appointments, and staff management requirements. The project uses demonstration data only; no real patient information is exposed.

Project Objectives

  • Understand healthcare administration workflows and requirements
  • Explore patient record management systems
  • Implement appointment scheduling functionality
  • Design staff management interfaces
  • Practice requirements definition and workflow analysis
