Overview

Alongside software development, I've done graphic design work for charities, community events, local businesses, and freelance clients. This covers social media content for Thrift For Good (a charity thrift shop), a Gaza humanitarian campaign, restaurant menu and logo design, and brand identity work for a private tuition company and a medical education platform.

Tools used: Canva, Adobe Express, and image editing tools depending on the deliverable. All work was created to brief for real organisations and events.
